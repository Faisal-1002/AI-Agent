# Qamar Freight Supplies - Product Catalogue, Edition 7

Qamar Freight sells warehouse and packaging supplies to its contract customers through the Supplies desk at the Thamra Inland Hub. This edition lists every stocked item with its product code, dimensions, weight and unit price. Dimensions are given as length x width x height in millimetres unless stated otherwise.

## Ordering terms

All prices are in SAR per unit and exclude value-added tax. The minimum order value is SAR 500. Orders above SAR 20,000 receive a 7 percent volume discount, applied to the whole order. The standard lead time is 5 working days; cold chain items (codes beginning QF-CLD and QF-LOG) have a lead time of 10 working days. Delivery is free on orders above SAR 3,000 within the regional store-delivery network.

## Pallets

QF-PAL-2209 - Thamra Standard Wooden Pallet. A heat-treated hardwood pallet measuring 1200 x 1000 x 150 mm and weighing 22 kg. It is rated for a dynamic load of 1,500 kg and a static load of 4,000 kg, with four-way entry for forklifts. Price: SAR 85 each, or SAR 78 each when bought in bundles of 20. This pallet replaces the discontinued QF-PAL-2101.

QF-PAL-2214 - Light Export Pallet. A moulded plastic pallet measuring 1200 x 800 x 140 mm and weighing 9.5 kg, with a dynamic load rating of 1,000 kg. Empty pallets nest inside each other, so a stack of 30 stands only 1.4 metres high. Price: SAR 62 each.

QF-PAL-3301 - Heavy-Duty Hygienic Pallet. A high-density plastic pallet measuring 1200 x 1000 x 160 mm and weighing 27 kg. It carries a dynamic load of 2,000 kg and a static load of 6,000 kg, and it is suitable for cold stores down to -30 °C. Price: SAR 240 each.

QF-PAL-1608 - Half Display Pallet. A plastic half pallet measuring 800 x 600 x 150 mm and weighing 6.2 kg, with a dynamic load of 500 kg, used for in-store promotional displays. Price: SAR 48 each.

## Crates and trolleys

QF-CRT-1105 - Foldable Crate. A collapsible plastic crate measuring 600 x 400 x 320 mm that folds flat to 80 mm high. It weighs 2.1 kg empty and holds up to 30 kg. Price: SAR 38 each.

QF-CRT-1120 - Ventilated Produce Crate. An open-sided crate for fruit and vegetables measuring 600 x 400 x 240 mm, weighing 1.6 kg and holding up to 20 kg. Price: SAR 29 each.

QF-TRL-0700 - Roll Cage Trolley. A steel roll cage measuring 700 x 800 x 1800 mm and weighing 48 kg, with a safe working load of 500 kg and four castors, two of them braked. Price: SAR 640 each.

## Cold chain

QF-CLD-0450 - Insulated Cold Box, 45 litres. External size 600 x 400 x 420 mm, weight 4.8 kg empty. With four conditioned gel packs it holds 2 to 8 °C for 36 hours. Price: SAR 310 each.

QF-CLD-0900 - Insulated Cold Box, 90 litres. External size 800 x 600 x 480 mm, weight 7.9 kg empty. With eight conditioned gel packs it holds 2 to 8 °C for 48 hours, the longest hold time in the range. Price: SAR 545 each.

QF-CLD-PCM6 - Phase-Change Gel Pack, set of six. Each pack weighs 0.9 kg and must be conditioned at 5 °C for 12 hours before use. Packs can be reused for up to 200 cycles. Price: SAR 96 per set.

QF-LOG-0005 - Temperature Data Logger. A small logger weighing 0.08 kg that records a reading every 5 minutes, stores 90 days of data and is accurate to within 0.3 °C. Each logger ships with a calibration certificate valid for 12 months. Price: SAR 189 each.

QF-SGN-0012 - Zone Signage Kit. Twelve floor and rack-end signs printed with zone codes in the same style used at the Thamra Inland Hub. Price: SAR 210 per kit.

QF-BND-CC01 - Cold Chain Starter Bundle. Two QF-CLD-0450 cold boxes, two sets of QF-CLD-PCM6 gel packs and one QF-LOG-0005 data logger, packed together for customers starting chilled home deliveries. Price: SAR 880 per bundle, a saving of SAR 121 against buying the items separately.

QF-BND-DK01 - Dock Consumables Bundle. Six rolls of QF-WRP-0023 stretch wrap, one pack of QF-CRN-0050 edge protectors and twenty QF-DUN-0912 air bags. Price: SAR 540 per bundle.

## Rental option

Racking bays and roll cage trolleys can also be rented by contract customers for a minimum of three months. Monthly rental is SAR 95 per QF-RCK-4200 bay and SAR 22 per QF-TRL-0700 trolley, and the installation charge for racking still applies. Rented items remain the property of Qamar Freight and are inspected on return; damage beyond normal wear is charged at the repair cost.

## Returns and warranty

Unused items in their original packaging may be returned within 30 days for a full credit. Plastic pallets and racking bays carry a 3-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Cold boxes and data loggers carry a 12-month warranty. Wooden pallets, wrap, strapping and air bags are consumables and are not covered by a warranty.
