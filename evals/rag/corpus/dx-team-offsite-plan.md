# Platform Team Offsite: Planning Notes

Brindlecoast Software Group, Platform and Reliability group
Organiser: Ilka, Engineering Programme Manager
Status: draft for team leads, version 2

## Why we are going

The last two quarters were dominated by incident work. On-call engineers handled more pages than in any previous period, and several planned improvements slipped. The offsite theme is "Fewer pages, calmer nights": we want to leave with an agreed plan to cut on-call pages by 30 percent over the next half-year, and with a clear owner for each change.

## Venue and timing

We have booked Orrin Lake Lodge for two nights, Wednesday to Friday, in the third week of next quarter. The lodge has one large meeting room that seats 40, three breakout rooms and reliable wired internet in the meeting spaces. Rooms are shared between two people unless someone asks for a single room on the sign-up form.

So far 34 people have confirmed: the platform engineers, the site reliability team, two product managers and two designers who work closely with the platform group.

## Travel

A chartered coach will leave the office car park at 08:30 on the Wednesday. The journey takes about two and a half hours, including a short stop. People who prefer to drive can claim mileage through the normal expenses process, but parking at the lodge is limited to twelve cars, so please tell Ilka in advance. Return transport on the Friday is still being arranged with the travel desk and will be confirmed once the final session length is agreed.

## Budget

The offsite budget is capped at 420 dollars per attendee. This covers accommodation, all meals at the lodge and the Thursday afternoon activities. Coach hire is paid from the central travel budget and does not count against the per-attendee cap. Anything beyond the cap, such as a room upgrade, is paid personally.

## Draft agenda

Wednesday afternoon: arrival, lunch, and a "state of the platform" session led by Oskar, Head of Platform, covering the incident numbers and the real cost of on-call load.

Thursday morning: three parallel workshops on alert hygiene, runbook quality, and the Lanternfish migration plan for the legacy job scheduler. Each workshop produces a short list of proposals with an owner and an effort estimate.

Thursday afternoon: an optional guided walk around the lake, or board games in the lodge library. Nothing in the evening is mandatory.

Friday morning: proposals are presented, the group votes on the top ten, and team leads commit to timelines. We wrap up before lunch.

## Sign-up and practicalities

Please complete the offsite form by the end of next week, including dietary needs and whether you need a single room. Laptops are welcome for the workshops, but Thursday afternoon is meant to be screen-free. On-call cover for the three days will be provided by the application teams, so nobody attending should be holding the pager.
