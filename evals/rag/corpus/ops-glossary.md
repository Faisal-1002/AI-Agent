# Qamar Freight - Internal Glossary of Terms and Acronyms

This glossary explains the short forms used in Qamar Freight procedures, reports and handheld terminal screens at the Thamra Inland Hub. Terms are listed in alphabetical order.

CXD (Cross-Dock): Freight that moves straight from an inbound door to an outbound door without being put away in storage. At the hub, CXD freight is staged at Doors 1 to 3 and must leave the building within six hours of arrival.

DLX (Dwell Limit Exceeded): A trailer that has stayed at a dock door for more than two hours. The gatehouse reports every DLX to the Duty Manager.

FEFO (First Expired, First Out): The picking rule for chilled and frozen goods, where the stock with the earliest expiry date is picked first.

GRN (Goods Received Note): The receiving record confirming what was delivered. Differences of more than two cartons per pallet need the shift supervisor's signature on the GRN.

HBE (High-Bay Endorsement): An add-on to the Class R reach truck certification that allows an operator to work in ZONE-B4 and in any racking above eight metres.

HO-3: The shift handover log, completed by the outgoing shift supervisor and countersigned by the incoming supervisor.

KHZ: The route code for the Port Kheraz shuttle, the busiest inbound lane at the hub.

Lantern: The internal code name for the warehouse management system replacement project.

MHE (Material Handling Equipment): Forklifts, reach trucks and powered pallet trucks. MHE-7 is the pre-use checklist completed before the first use of a truck on each shift.

NSF (No-Show Flag): A flag recorded by the gatehouse against a carrier that does not turn up for a booked dock appointment. Three NSFs in one quarter put the carrier on the Commercial team's review list.

OTD (On-Time Delivery): The share of deliveries that reach the customer inside the agreed delivery window. Reported monthly to the board.

PID (Pallet ID): The ten-character label code, beginning with the letter P, applied to every accepted pallet at receiving.

QDR (Quarantine Disposition Request): The record raised for each pallet placed in ZONE-Q1. It tracks the Quality Lead's decision to release, rework, return or write off the stock.

RTW (Ready To Wave): The status given to an order when every order line has stock allocated in a pickable zone, so the order can be released into the next pick wave.

SLT (Senior Leadership Team): The Chief Executive Officer and the heads of each function. The SLT meets every Monday and reviews the previous week's OTD and TXE figures.

TXE (Temperature Excursion Event): Any temperature reading outside a zone's band for long enough to trigger an alarm. A TXE lasting more than 30 minutes sends all affected pallets to ZONE-Q1.

WCR (Wave Close Report): The summary of picks, shorts and late orders sent to the Duty Manager within 30 minutes of each wave closing.

ZONE codes: Every storage area at the hub has a code made of the word ZONE, a letter for the storage type and a number, for example ZONE-A1 for fast-moving ambient pick faces or ZONE-F1 for the chilled store. The letter Q is reserved for quarantine and R for returns.
