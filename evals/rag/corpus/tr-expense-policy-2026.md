# Falak Software Expense Policy 2026

**Document:** FIN-POL-007, revision 4
**Owner:** Rania, Finance Director
**Effective:** 1 January 2026

> This policy SUPERSEDES the Falak Software Expense Policy 2025 (FIN-POL-007, revision 3) in full. Any expense incurred on or after 1 January 2026 is reimbursed under this version only. Limits have changed in sections 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 11, so please read those sections again even if you know the previous edition well.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy sets out which business costs Falak Software reimburses and the maximum amounts that apply. It covers permanent employees, fixed-term staff on the Falak payroll and interns travelling on approved company business. All amounts are in Saudi riyals (SAR) and include VAT unless a section says otherwise. Costs above a limit are not reimbursed unless an exception has been approved in advance, as described in section 2.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

Line managers may approve individual claims of up to 5,000 SAR. A claim above that amount needs a second approval from the Finance Director. Any trip expected to cost more than 20,000 SAR in total must be approved by the Chief Operating Officer before anything is booked. Exceptions to the limits in this policy are requested through the expense tool before the cost is incurred, never afterwards.

## 3. Accommodation

For domestic trips, hotel costs are reimbursed up to 750 SAR per night, including breakfast where the hotel offers it. For international trips, the limit is 900 SAR per night. If the event venue hotel costs more than the limit, you may book it with your manager's written approval attached to the claim. Stays with friends or family are not reimbursed as accommodation, but the daily meal allowance in section 8 still applies.

## 4. Air Travel

Flights are booked through the Travel Desk in the Falak portal. Economy class is the default for all journeys. Premium economy may be booked for a single leg longer than 4 hours. Business class may be booked only when the scheduled flight time of a single leg is more than 8 hours. Seat selection and one checked bag are covered; paid upgrades bought by the traveller are not.

## 5. Ground Transport and Mileage

Taxis, ride-hailing and airport trains are reimbursed at cost when used for business travel. If you drive your own car on company business, Falak pays 0.95 SAR per kilometre, measured from your office or your home, whichever is closer to the destination. Airport parking is covered for up to 5 days per trip. Car rental needs prior approval and should be the smallest suitable category.

## 6. Training and Conferences

Each employee has an annual training budget of 7,500 SAR for courses, certification exams and conference tickets. The budget is per calendar year and unused amounts do not carry over. Travel to a conference follows the accommodation, air travel and meal rules in this policy and is not deducted from the training budget.

## 7. Client Entertainment

Business meals with clients or partners are reimbursed up to 250 SAR per guest, counting Falak attendees as guests. The claim must name the client organisation and the business purpose of the meeting. Gifts to clients are not covered by this policy; they follow the separate Gifts and Hospitality Standard.

## 8. Meals and Daily Allowance

For each day of an overnight business trip, employees receive a daily meal allowance of 200 SAR on domestic trips and 260 SAR on international trips. The allowance is paid with the trip claim and meal receipts are not needed. Meals already provided by a hotel, conference or client are deducted at 60 SAR each. Day trips without an overnight stay do not qualify for the allowance.

## 9. Home Office Equipment

Employees on a hybrid or remote contract may claim a home office allowance of 2,000 SAR for a desk, chair, lighting or similar items. The allowance can be claimed once in any three-year period. Laptops, monitors and docks are supplied by IT from the equipment catalogue and are not bought through this allowance.

## 10. Mobile and Internet

Employees whose role requires them to be reachable outside working hours, including everyone on an on-call rota, receive a mobile and internet allowance of 150 SAR per month. The allowance is paid with salary and does not need receipts.

## 11. Receipts and Submission Deadlines

An itemised receipt is required for every expense above 50 SAR. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date, or of the last day of the trip for travel claims. Late claims are rejected unless the Finance Director approves an exception in writing.

## 12. Items That Are Not Reimbursed

Falak does not reimburse traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment such as films or minibar items, upgrades bought without approval, travel for family members, personal software subscriptions, or costs already refunded by an airline or insurer. Questions about this policy go to the Finance team through the expense tool.
