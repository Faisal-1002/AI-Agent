# Incident Report QF-INC-0457 - Dock Leveller Failure at Dock 7

Site: Thamra Inland Hub. Reported in Week 32 (Tuesday, Dawn shift start).
Incident level: Level 2 (product and equipment loss, no injury).
Investigation lead: Lina, Head of Quality.
Report approved by: Rania, Head of Warehouse Operations.

## Summary

During the unloading of a chilled trailer at Dock 7, the lip of the dock leveller dropped while a counterbalance forklift was crossing it. The front wheel of the forklift lodged in the gap and the pallet being carried tipped onto the trailer floor. The operator was not hurt. Because the dock was then out of use, the trailer doors stayed open for far longer than the cold chain rules allow and the whole load had to be quarantined. Three pallets were written off, with a stock loss of SAR 27,600, and Dock 7 was out of service for four days.

## Timeline

05:47 - A trailer from Port Kheraz carrying 14 pallets of chilled dairy desserts for Tellara Fresh Markets docks at Dock 7. The trailer temperature record shows 3.8 °C, inside the agreed range.

05:55 - The Night shift receiving team starts unloading with a Class C counterbalance forklift.

06:00 - Shift change. The handover between the Night and Dawn shift supervisors starts at Door 9. The Night shift HO-3 does not mention that the Dock 7 leveller had been slow to rise twice during the night.

06:09 - While the forklift is crossing with the fourth pallet, the leveller lip drops by about 12 cm. The front wheel lodges in the gap and the pallet tips over inside the trailer. The operator stops the truck and steps down unhurt.

06:12 - The Dawn shift supervisor cordons off Dock 7. The operator is stood down from driving in line with the manual, and the forklift is tagged out of service for inspection.

06:31 - The on-call facilities technician arrives and isolates the leveller. The remaining pallets cannot be moved through Dock 7.

06:44 - The team waits for a decision on whether to move the trailer. The trailer doors remain open throughout.

07:01 - The trailer is moved to Dock 5. By this point the doors had been open for 74 minutes, and probe readings on the top pallets show a product temperature of 9.1 °C.

07:20 - All 14 pallets are placed on hold in ZONE-Q1 and a Quarantine Disposition Request is raised for each one.

Next day - After core temperature checks, the Quality Lead releases 11 pallets to stock. The tipped pallet and the two pallets nearest the open doors, 3 pallets in total, are written off.

## Root cause

The hydraulic lift valve on the Dock 7 leveller was worn and leaking, which allowed the lip to sag and then drop under load. The leveller's six-monthly preventive maintenance was six weeks overdue. When the facilities maintenance contract moved to a new contractor in Week 21, the preventive maintenance calendar was not transferred into the new contractor's system, so no service visit was scheduled.

## Contributing factors

First, the Night shift had noticed the leveller rising slowly but reported it only in a message to the maintenance desk. It was not written on HO-3, so the Dawn shift did not know about it. Second, the trailer doors were left open for 74 minutes, well beyond the 20-minute limit for chilled trailers, while the team waited for a decision on moving the trailer. Third, there is no alarm to warn the supervisor when a chilled trailer's doors have been open too long.

## Corrective actions

CA-1 - Replace the hydraulic lift valves on all 12 dock levellers at the hub and pressure-test each one. Owner: Karim, Facilities Manager. Due: Week 36.

CA-2 - Add a mandatory equipment defects section to the handover log HO-3, so that any equipment behaving unusually must be recorded. Owner: Rania, Head of Warehouse Operations. Due: Week 34.

CA-3 - Rebuild the preventive maintenance calendar in the new contractor's system and audit every dock asset against it. Owner: Karim, Facilities Manager. Due: Week 35.

CA-4 - Give a dock-crossing refresher session to all Class C operators, including how to check a leveller before driving onto it. Owner: Nadia, Training Coordinator. Due: Week 38.

CA-5 - Install door-open alarms at every dock that alert the shift supervisor after 15 minutes when a chilled trailer is docked. Owner: Omar, Head of Technology. Due: Week 40.

## Lessons for all shifts

An equipment problem that is only mentioned in a message is not handed over. If a truck, leveller or door behaves unusually, write it on HO-3. If a chilled trailer cannot be unloaded, close the doors first and decide what to do next with the doors closed.
